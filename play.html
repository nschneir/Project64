CREDITS

The demos are ours. The Commodore 64 they run on is not — it is three pieces of other people’s work, loaded at runtime and not bundled here.

vc64web — the emulator in the frame above A WebAssembly port by mithrendal, served from a maintainer-owned fork so this page and the emulator share an origin.

VirtualC64 — the emulator vc64web is a port of By Dirk W. Hoffmann. GPL-3.0

MEGA65 open-roms — the KERNAL, BASIC and character ROMs it boots on A free/libre re-implementation by Paul Gardner-Stephen and Roman Standzikowski (FeralChild64). The PXL character font is by Retrofan, included with permission. LGPL-3.0-or-later · parts of BASIC additionally MIT © Microsoft Corporation

These are not Commodore’s ROMs. No Commodore ROM image is hosted or distributed by either repository, and none is sent to your browser. That is also why there is no 1541 drive ROM here at all, and why each demo is loaded as a .prg flashed straight into RAM rather than from a disk image.
