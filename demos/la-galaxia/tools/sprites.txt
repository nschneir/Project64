#
# The 16x16 arcade shapes are centred in the C64's 24x21 box, so a sprite
# coordinate and the art coordinate never drift apart: hires art occupies
# columns 4-19, multicolour art columns 2-9 (of 12 double-wide pixels).
#
# Emitted order is block order.  Hires first (blocks 128-133), then
# multicolour (blocks 133-148).  Original art throughout.
